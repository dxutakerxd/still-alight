When can I get Still Alight?+

Still Alight is being prepared for Google Play. There isn't a public download or confirmed release date yet. This page will link to the store when it's available.

Which phones will it work on?+

The current app is built for Android 8.0 and later on compatible phones with OpenGL ES 3.0. It is designed for portrait use. Live wallpaper placement depends on your device and launcher.

Can I make the candle my own?+

Yes. Choose Classic, Triple wick, or Oval wood wick, then Vanilla Blossom, Lavender Fields, or Cozy Cabin. Design a label with your words, drawings, stickers, or selected photos, and preview it on the 3D jar.

Where are my memories and photos stored?+

The current prerelease stores remembrance details, labels, and imported media in private app storage on your phone. The release is expected to add advertising and purchase services; their data practices will be documented before launch. Read the privacy policy and local data removal guide for the current details.

Will the app have ads or a paid option?+

Advertising through AdMob and an optional paid ad-free version using RevenueCat are planned for release. They aren't integrated in the current preview build. Pricing and final purchase details haven't been announced.

Is it a timer, a widget, or a live wallpaper?+

It is a candle app with quiet-session timers and an optional live wallpaper. A home-screen widget is not currently included. Timers follow the phone's clock; they aren't alarms and do not wake the device or send a notification when finished.
