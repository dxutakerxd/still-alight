## A — Quiet Flame

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original logo concept presentation for the Still Alight Android candle app and its website.
Brand context: Still Alight is a calm, tactile 3D candle app with personal remembrance candles, custom labels, quiet ambient sound and live wallpaper. The website's approved visual direction is a warm candlelit room with rich espresso brown, warm ivory, muted olive and restrained honey-gold. Brand should feel quietly hopeful, thoughtful, warm and approachable.
Output: a polished landscape identity exploration board, approximately 3:2. Flat vector-like design, precise edges and generous whitespace. Upper two thirds show one large original symbol and a beautifully spaced wordmark. Lower third shows three compact applications of THAT SAME symbol: solid black on ivory, reversed ivory on espresso, and a rounded-square Android app icon. Consistent symbol in every application, simple enough to read at 24px. Plain warm ivory background; no photographic mockups, no paper textures, no 3D extrusion, no gradient shading, no gold foil. A slim small label at the top identifies the direction.
The brand name must read exactly "Still Alight". No "by Satzquatch", no publisher byline, no tagline, no extra brand names. Do not imitate a known logo. The only other allowed text is the direction label specified below. Design an actual concise, reproducible identity rather than a decorative illustration.
Direction label (verbatim): "A / QUIET FLAME".
Design direction: Distill a candle into an unmistakable compact symbol: a single softly asymmetric teardrop flame above a tiny wick and a low open-top vessel made from one elegantly rounded U shape. Bold enough for an app icon, balanced negative space, with a subtle flat base suggesting stillness. Use just two or three clean forms. Main mark espresso with a muted honey-gold flame. Pair with a refined, warm, slightly old-style serif wordmark, title case, beautifully kerned. It should feel like a quiet independent design studio, not a spa stock logo. No leaves, circles of rays, thin ornamental lines or realistic candle drawing.
```
