# Still Alight brand kit

Approved direction: **A — Quiet Flame**, selected 29 September 2026.
Reserved secondary identity: **C — SA / Alight Monogram**.

## Main identity: Quiet Flame

The candle symbol is the primary identity for the website and browser icon. These SVGs are a clean, scalable implementation of the selected concept.

- [Single-color light symbol](../public/assets/brand/quiet-flame-mono-light.svg)

- [Dark horizontal logo](primary/quiet-flame-lockup-dark.svg)
- [Light horizontal logo](primary/quiet-flame-lockup-light.svg)

The horizontal SVG logos retain editable text, using Georgia bold with Times New Roman/serif fallback, like the website. Their letterforms can vary by installed fonts. The symbol SVGs are self-contained paths and render consistently without fonts. The browser icon is a web asset; Android launcher and Google Play store graphics have not been replaced.

## Secondary identity: SA

- [Transparent espresso SA monogram PNG](secondary/sa-monogram-dark.png)
- [Approved monogram concept and color applications](references/sa-monogram-approved.png)

The isolated PNG was prepared with built-in imagegen from the approved C concept. Keep it as a secondary mark for other uses. Use this dark version on light backgrounds, preserve its alpha channel, and do not stretch it. It has not replaced Quiet Flame on the website.

## Colors and spacing

| Color | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Espresso | `#392619` | Dark symbols and wordmarks |
| Ivory | `#F7F1E5` | Light symbols and quiet backgrounds |
| Honey | `#DCA331` | Flame accent |
| Olive | `#626344` | Icon background |

Keep clear space of at least one flame-width around an isolated symbol. Use the one-color versions where two colors are impractical. Keep marks flat and avoid adding glows or drop shadows.
